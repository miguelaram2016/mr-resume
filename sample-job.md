# Sample Job Description: SaaS Operations Manager

**Company:** TechFlow SaaS (Austin-based startup)  
**Location:** Austin, TX (Hybrid)  
**Salary:** $90,000 - $120,000  
**Job Type:** Full-time

---

## About the Role

We're looking for an Operations Manager to join our growing SaaS company. You'll be responsible for streamlining business processes, managing client workflows, and ensuring operational excellence across our platform.

## Responsibilities

- **Process Optimization:** Design and implement efficient workflows to reduce operational friction
- **Client Onboarding:** Manage customer implementation and ensure smooth handoffs
- **Vendor Management:** Coordinate with third-party service providers and negotiate contracts
- **Financial Operations:** Oversee AP/AR, invoicing, and revenue reconciliation
- **Team Coordination:** Liaise between technical teams and business stakeholders
- **Compliance:** Ensure adherence to industry regulations and internal policies
- **Analytics:** Track KPIs and provide actionable insights to leadership

## Requirements

- 3+ years of operations or project management experience in tech/SaaS
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Operations, or related field
- Strong analytical skills with proficiency in Excel/Google Sheets
- Experience with CRM platforms (HubSpot, Salesforce) 
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
- Experience optimizing workflows and reducing operational costs
- Familiarity with API integrations and automation tools

## Preferred Skills

- Technical background or understanding of software development
- Experience with billing/_subscription platforms (Stripe, Chargebee)
- Knowledge of data analytics tools (SQL, Tableau, Looker)
- Startup experience preferred
- Project management certifications (PMP, Six Sigma) a plus

## Benefits

- Competitive salary + equity
- Health, dental, vision insurance
- Flexible PTO
- Remote/hybrid work
- Professional development budget
